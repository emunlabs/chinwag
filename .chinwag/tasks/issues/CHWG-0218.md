Parent feature tracking end-to-end delivery of **Unattended Commit-Pinned Cross-Model Review (`design-v2`, SHA-256 `3039559d8a8c4181cd319a9fab7df57f1b4ff849551d60edb4936de45fd8b73e`)** and live operational proof/handoff to **Chinwag Delivery-2** (`CHWG-0189` / issue `#19`).

### Product Promise
Engineers request a review **once** on a commit checkpoint (`chinwag review` or one click in the local web app), leave or close the window, and return to a durable stored result (`Review passed`, `Changes requested`, `Action needed on this change` strictly for code/scope/binary/intent decisions, or `Review did not complete — assigned to project operator` for all service/route/policy faults) without babysitting model settings, token caps, routine test runs, worker restarts, or receipt transport.
