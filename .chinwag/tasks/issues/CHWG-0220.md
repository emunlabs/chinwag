### Exact Scope & Deliverables
1. **Three-Entity Identity & Four-Scope SQLite Schema (§ 3.1, § 10.1(1)):** WAL + `FULL` synchronous authority tables for `review_cases`, permanent same-ID `request_intents` (rejecting mutated input bindings on reused client IDs, freezing JCS semantic fingerprint including `test_evidence_bundle_sha256`), immutable terminal `execution_jobs` (`<= 1` paid generation), `raw_redacted_provider_receipt`, four-scope ledger (`per_attempt`, `per_candidate`, `per_case_task`, `per_project_epoch`), append-only late settlement/result amendments, and `notification_outbox`.
2. **Complete Two-Axis Context Tiers & Overhead-First Priority (§ 5.1):** Classify `(finalized_wire_tokens, finalized_wire_bytes)` into `COMPACT` (`<=4,000` & `<=16,384`), `STANDARD` (`<=16,000` & `<=65,536`), `UNAVAILABLE_SYSTEM_OVERHEAD_EXCEEDED` (mandatory-policy envelope validated first `>8,000` tok or `>32,768` B, even when candidate is also large), and `ACTION_NEEDED_CANDIDATE_TOO_LARGE`, reserving `B_input + 32,768`.
3. **Versioned Usage Normalizers & Settlement Precision (§ 5.3):** Exact single-count `gemini-v1beta-norm-v1` (plus held schemas), `RELEASED_UNSENT` strictly pre-`DISPATCHED`, qualified `SETTLED_ZERO_CONFIRMED` vs unqualified `429`/`5xx` `UNKNOWN_UNMETERED`, full actual overrun debit + profile quarantine, and uniform single-exposure `UNKNOWN_UNMETERED` (`gate_eligible = false`) on missing, negative, or inconsistent usage fields.
4. **Atomic `RESPONSE_CAPTURED_FINALIZING` & Universal `ReviewResultAttestationV2` (§ 6.1–6.2):** Single-transaction redacted visible-response + settlement commit, zero-call deterministic local replay, untrusted model JSON validator (`PASS` with `BLOCKING`/`HIGH` or `missing_context` -> `INCOMPLETE_MALFORMED_MODEL_OUTPUT`), `Ed25519` host signing of all terminal outcomes (explicit `null` + reason for unavailable early fields), and `<= 1` known-settled `OPERATOR_RECOVERY_SUCCESSOR` enforcement (blocking paid replacements while an original generation remains `UNKNOWN`).
