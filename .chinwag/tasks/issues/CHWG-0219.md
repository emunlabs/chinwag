### Exact Scope & Deliverables
1. **`PG-1` Existing Hook & Workflow Trace (`crates/chinwag-review-packet/docs/pg1-hook-trace.md`):** Trace exact integration points across `task_acceptance.rs`, `task_scheduler.rs`, `execution_binding.rs`, `hosted_agent.rs`, and existing `chinwag-ci` runner grants/idempotency, validating `W_checks` and same-ID intake deduplication.
2. **Base-Lineage Policy Loader (§ 4.1):** Load governing `chinwag.workflow.yaml`, `required_test_suites`, and `binary_review_exempt` rules strictly from pinned `base_commit_id` and versioned operator snapshots; keep candidate edits to workflow/agent dotfiles strictly inside untrusted `canonical_diff`.
3. **Signed Author Provenance Resolver (§ 4.2):** Derive `HUMAN_ONLY`, `SINGLE_AI_FAMILY(F)`, `MIXED_AI_FAMILIES(S)`, or fail-closed `UNKNOWN_UNVERIFIED` from verified commit/dispatch records across `base_commit_id..candidate_commit_id`.
4. **Config-Independent Canonical Diff, Tombstones & Binary Scope (§ 4.4):** Deterministic unified diff (`context = 3`, lexicographic byte-path sort, modes, create/delete headers, `(old_path, new_path)` rename records) invariant across `.gitconfig`/`.jjconfig`; intact global + `(old_path ∪ new_path)` tombstones; explicit binary hash/size metadata (`ACTION_NEEDED_UNREVIEWABLE_BINARY_ARTIFACT` unless `binary_review_exempt: true`).
